Lain OS is an open-source framework for autonomous AI agents, and this app is the chat with Lain herself.

• Talk to Lain on the models you already use — free models included.
• Share any text or link from another app to "Ask Lain" and a new chat opens with it typed in.
• Long-press the icon for a new chat, the Lain OS desktop and the roadmap.
• Your conversations are yours: manage or delete them in the chat.
